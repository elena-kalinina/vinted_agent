Most productivity apps punish you for being human. Miss a day? Streak broken. Skip a workout? Red warning. I built something different.

Adaptive Routines is an AI-powered app where you describe a goal in plain language -- "I want to practice LeetCode for 30 minutes every day at 5 PM for 3 months" -- and the AI doesn't just give you a wall of text. It generates a full syllabus of progressive, contextual sessions and drops them straight into your calendar. Day 1: Arrays. Day 2: Two Pointers. Each one with a specific topic and a 5-minute "Minimum Viable Routine" for the days when life gets in the way. Because it will.

That's the part I'm most proud of: the "Life Happened" button. When you can't show up fully, the app doesn't guilt you -- it gives you three calm options to adapt. Downgrade to the 5-minute version. Push it back. Reshuffle your week. And every single one of those choices earns you Resilience Points. No streaks. No punishment. Zero guilt. Because reaching long-term goals isn't about perfect consistency -- it's about how gracefully you recover from the days that don't go to plan.

Built with React, Supabase, and Gemini 3.1 Pro. Would love your feedback -- and if this resonates, I'd love to hear how you handle disruptions in your own routines.
